# Working notes: shared league with friends (stage 5)

Not a design yet. Lines marked **[my call]** are Claude's proposals the owner has not agreed to.

## How time advances (owner, 2026-11-23)

- Both ways are offered and picked per league:
  - Commissioner advances: one person moves the league forward when everyone is ready.
  - Scheduled: the league advances by itself at set times; a GM who has not acted gets his assistant GM's choices.

Proposed details **[my call]**:

- The league can switch between the two at any time (for example scheduled in season, commissioner-run at the draft and deadline).
- A "ready" mark per GM, and the league can advance early when everyone is ready.

## Trades between human GMs (owner, 2026-11-23)

- A per-league setting: no check (the default), commissioner approval, or a league vote where other GMs get a day to object.

## Watching games (owner, 2026-11-23)

- Each GM watches on his own time, with the result hidden until he watches or skips. A game is simmed once and replayed from its seed.
- Watching together live (a watch party) is a possible later addition, not a priority: the owner would rather the effort go elsewhere.
